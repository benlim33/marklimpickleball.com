Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who are lessons for?

Coaching is designed for beginners through experienced recreational players. Mark will tailor each session to your goals and current level.

What should I bring?

Bring a paddle, comfortable court shoes, water, and a willingness to learn. Details for your specific session can be confirmed when booking.

Where do lessons take place?

Court location and availability will be confirmed when you book. Reach out with your preferred area and schedule.

What happens if it rains?

Outdoor sessions may need to be rescheduled for weather. Mark will coordinate a new time directly with you.
